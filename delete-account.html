Request account deletion

If you no longer want to use VALO, you can request deletion of your account and associated personal data here—even if you have uninstalled the app.

Send a deletion request

Enter the email address linked to your VALO account. We will contact you at that address to verify the request before taking action.

Submitting this form does not immediately delete your account. We will verify the request first.

Deletion request records: Information submitted through this form is used only to verify and process your account deletion request. Once the request has been completed, the submission will be deleted.

What happens next?

1
We receive your request

Your request is sent to VALO support for review.

2
We verify that it is yours

We will reply to the submitted email address. You must confirm the request, and we may ask for additional verification if needed.

3
We process deletion

After verification, we will delete your account and the associated data described below, subject to applicable retention requirements.

4
You receive confirmation

We will email you when processing is complete.

What data is deleted?

After a request is verified, VALO intends to delete the account and associated personal data, including as applicable:

  • Firebase Authentication account and VALO profile
  • Profile details and preferences
  • Profile pictures and uploaded gallery photos
  • Presence information and other account-linked records
  • Associated chat data, subject to the retention policy below
What happens to your data?

Once your account deletion request has been verified and processed, your VALO account and associated personal data will be permanently deleted. VALO does not retain your personal account data after deletion.

Processing timeframe: within 30 days after successful verification
