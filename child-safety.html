Child Safety Standards

VALO is committed to providing a safer dating and social environment. We prohibit child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), sexual grooming of minors, and any content or behaviour that exploits or endangers children.

Adults only: VALO is exclusively for people aged 18 and older. Anyone under 18 is prohibited from creating an account or using the service.

1. Zero Tolerance for Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation

VALO strictly prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). The following conduct is not permitted:

  • Creating, uploading, requesting, sharing, distributing, or promoting child sexual abuse material (CSAM).
  • Sexual grooming, sexual exploitation, or sexual solicitation of a minor.
  • Sextortion, trafficking, coercion, or any conduct that sexually exploits or endangers a child.
  • Using profiles, private messages, images, or other VALO features to facilitate child abuse or exploitation.
  • Helping a minor access VALO or knowingly misrepresenting a minor's age.

Do not download, save, copy, forward, or share suspected child sexual abuse material. Report the account or concern through the available reporting channels instead.

2. Age Restriction

VALO is an 18+ service. Users must provide truthful information and must not use VALO if they are under 18.

Accounts reasonably suspected of belonging to minors may be investigated and suspended or removed as appropriate.

3. How to Report a Safety Concern

Users can report suspicious or harmful behaviour through the Report User feature in VALO where available. Select the reason that best describes the concern and provide relevant details to help us investigate.

Concerns may include:

  • Suspected users under 18.
  • Suspected child sexual abuse or exploitation.
  • Sexually inappropriate content or behaviour.
  • Harassment, threats, impersonation, or other harmful activity.

Do not attach or send suspected child sexual abuse material. Provide relevant usernames, profile details, or other non-explicit information that can help identify the concern.

If the in-app reporting feature is unavailable, contact our child-safety contact listed below.

4. Review and Enforcement

VALO reviews submitted reports and takes appropriate action based on the information available and the seriousness of the concern.

Possible actions include:

  • Removing content that violates our standards.
  • Restricting or suspending an account.
  • Permanently terminating an account.
  • Escalating suspected child-safety cases where appropriate.

Reports involving suspected child sexual abuse or exploitation are treated as urgent. We assess reports before deciding on enforcement, except where immediate protective action is necessary.

5. Cooperation with Authorities

VALO will comply with applicable laws and reporting obligations concerning child sexual abuse and exploitation. Where required or appropriate, we will report relevant cases to competent law-enforcement, child-protection, or designated reporting authorities.

We may preserve relevant information where legally permitted or required and cooperate with appropriate authorities in accordance with applicable law.

7. Updates to These Standards

We may update these standards as VALO's features, safety practices, or legal obligations change. The current version will be available on this page.

8. Immediate Danger

If a child or another person is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or the appropriate child-protection authority. VALO's reporting channels are not a substitute for emergency assistance.
